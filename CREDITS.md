## Image-specific notices

- **Comet 67P:** ESA/Rosetta/NAVCAM, [CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/igo/). The bundled resized `public/photos/comet.webp` is provided under those same terms. [ESA release and reuse explanation](https://blogs.esa.int/rosetta/2014/11/04/rosetta-navcam-images-now-available-under-a-creative-commons-licence/).
- **Jupiter:** enhanced image by Kevin M. Gill (CC BY), based on images provided courtesy of NASA/JPL-Caltech/SwRI/MSSS. The original NASA archive description identifies the processing credit and CC BY notice. [PIA22946](https://images.nasa.gov/details/PIA22946). The bundled `public/photos/jupiter.webp` is resized and converted to WebP.
- **Milky Way:** image courtesy of the Earth Science and Remote Sensing Unit, NASA Johnson Space Center. Astronaut photograph **iss073e0982261**, taken aboard the ISS. [NASA astronaut imagery FAQ](https://eol.jsc.nasa.gov/FAQ/). This shows our galaxy from within, with Earth and station hardware in the foreground.
- **Orion Nebula:** NASA, ESA, T. Megeath (University of Toledo), M. Robberto (STScI). Visible/ultraviolet/infrared observations are combined using representative colors. [Original SVS image and credits](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/30775/).
- Other images retain the credits in the table and original archive records. See [NASA media guidance](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) and [SVS reuse guidance](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/). This independent educational game does not imply agency endorsement.

The photo archive records, actual download URLs and descriptions are retained in `art-source/photo-metadata.json`. Original pixel-art atlas prompts and saved files are described in `art-source/PROMPTS.md`. The BlueTTS engine license and audio generation provenance are in `scripts/hebrew/`.
