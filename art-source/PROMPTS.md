## Main atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: pixel-art sprite atlas for Bar's bilingual space explorer. ONE atlas with EXACTLY FIVE COLUMNS and FOUR ROWS, 20 equal square cells, no dividers, every sprite entirely contained with ample margin in its own cell, all centered. True lovingly crafted 16-bit pixel art, crisp square pixels, deliberate chunky pixel clusters, rich limited palettes, warm highlights and saturated jewel colors, no smooth vector shapes, no antialiasing, no labels or text. TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND in all empty spaces. Row 1 left to right: golden glowing Sun with visible solar texture (no face); gray cratered Mercury; pale creamy yellow cloudy Venus; blue Earth with green continents; rusty orange Mars with a small pale polar cap. Row 2: large creamy striped Jupiter with a red storm spot; golden Saturn with broad tilted icy rings fully inside cell; pale turquoise Uranus with faint thin tilted ring; deep blue Neptune; tan icy heart-marked Pluto. Row 3: gray cratered Moon; irregular dark rocky asteroid Bennu, no rings; irregular icy comet with bright blue-white coma and a long sparkling tail; beautiful compact blue-white star cluster (Pleiades), isolated points with blue reflection clouds; luminous pink and turquoise Orion Nebula with stars. Row 4: Milky Way seen from within as a glowing diagonal band of many tiny stars; Andromeda seen from outside as a tilted elongated spiral galaxy with a golden center; a delightful white and coral explorer rocket with blue window and golden engine flame; a friendly young boy astronaut with tousled brown hair visible in a round helmet, white suit and small blue backpack, full body; a tiny whimsical orbital space telescope with gold foil and blue solar panels. Educational objects recognizable, no faces on planets, no logos, no typography, no UI. Use a consistent crisp pixel-art game sprite style, largest sprites occupy at most 80 percent of each cell so Saturn rings and comet tail are never cut off.

## Ring corrections

Create a transparent pixel-art sprite atlas with exactly TWO equal square cells, side by side, landscape 2:1 aspect ratio. Left cell ONLY Saturn, warm gold striped planet with full wide tilted rings. Right cell ONLY Uranus, pale turquoise sphere with subtle surface shading and a very faint thin ring. Crisp detailed 16-bit pixel art, limited rich palettes, warm highlights, no antialiasing. Each planet with all its rings must fit FULLY inside its own square cell with at least 15% transparent margin on EVERY side. Saturn MUST NOT cross into Uranus's cell. Absolutely no overlap between the two sprites. No stars, text, letters, faces or backgrounds. Match the style of the provided space sprite atlas, but redraw only Saturn and Uranus with their entire ring systems contained safely in their respective cells.
